Reproducibility of the SOM map layout. (A) Map-layout concordance: Spearman correlation between the grid distances separating the best matching units of all event pairs in two SOM fits, for same-grid pairs of the 250 full-data maps and of the 200 grouped-resample maps. For reference, the aligned two-dimensional PCA scores of the same resamples were quantised onto the matching SOM grid and compared in the same way, and event distances in the continuous PCA plane are shown last. (B) Mean event-level Jaccard overlap of map neighbours (events mapped to the same or an adjacent unit) between two fits, beside the overlap expected after relabelling events at random.
